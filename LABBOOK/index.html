Annotations to this article

In the electrophoresis images,shorthand labels are used for DNA strands and various assemblies. The annotations are explained below.

Annotations for RDT-related tetrahedral structures

①A、A1、A2、A3 –the A-face of RDT and its three component strands.

②B、B1、B2、B3 –the B-face of RDT and its three component strands.

③C、C1、C2、C3 –the C-face of RDT and its three component strands.

④D、D1、D2、D3 –the D-face of RDT and its three component strands.

⑤1+2、2+3、1+3–pairwise combinations of strands within one face. In single-face-specific electrophoresis characterizations,the strands loaded belong to the corresponding face;in all other cases,the strands used are from face A.

⑥RDT denotes the rigid DNA tetrahedron,the suffix 0.1X, etc. indicates the dilution ratio applied when the four faces are combined for tetrahedron assembly,for example, 0.1X means mixing equimolar A, B, C and D to 10 µL and adding 90 µL 1× TE buffer. If sequential dilutions are shown,e.g. 1× to 0.2×,it means the 1× assembly is simply diluted to the DNA concentration corresponding to 0.2× and left to stand for the indicated time.

⑦GS1、GS2、GS3、GS4 designate the four strands of the simple tetrahedron; the prefix G in the last three strands indicates that each contains a vertex sequence capable of forming a G-quadruplex.

⑧TDN-1、TDN-2、TDN-3、TDN-4 same meaning as ⑦.

⑨TDN–simple tetrahedron.

Time-and-temperature labels appended to each sample indicate that the assembly was carried out using the specified annealing program and that the product was subsequently stored at the stated temperature for the duration shown (if a time is given, it corresponds to the annotated value).

For future experiments, it is best to refer to the buffer formulation in the 2022 mitochondrial-interference paper.
